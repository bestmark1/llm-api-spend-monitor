Where the number came from

Official

Reported by the provider's own billing API — cost, and often tokens and models.

Estimated

Derived, and labelled. DeepSeek publishes no cost history, so spend is computed from saved balance decreases — and says so on the card.

—Unavailable

And the state of the card · 3 of 12

Up to date

The latest provider data is available.

Incomplete

The provider returned only part of the report. Flagged, not patched.

Not connected

No credential yet, so no metrics. Never joins the total as a zero.

The idea

It knows what it doesn't know.

Every dashboard promises one clean total. To print it, they have to invent the parts nobody reports. Spender refuses: where the number is derived it says estimated, shows the method, and keeps it out of the official total.
