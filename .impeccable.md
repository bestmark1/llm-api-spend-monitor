## Design Context

### Users

Independent developers and technically confident individuals who use several LLM APIs. They open a menu bar utility for a fast financial check between work sessions, not for prolonged accounting. Their core job is to understand current official spend, tokens, balances, freshness, and provider problems without visiting four dashboards. Credentials and financial data must feel private and trustworthy.

### Brand Personality

Calm, precise, and natively premium. The product should create quiet confidence rather than urgency: factual language, restrained motion, and clear provenance. Its personality comes from unusually honest capability states and polished macOS interactions, not AI-themed decoration.

### Aesthetic Direction

An OpenUsage-inspired macOS utility with a distinct "financial calm at a glance" direction. Use native SwiftUI/AppKit materials, SF typography and symbols, system light/dark/high-contrast behavior, compact editorial hierarchy, and generous separation between summary and provider detail. Provider color is reserved for meaningful data encoding. Avoid neon AI gradients, permanent glass effects, oversized branding, decorative sparklines, nested cards, and fake financial precision.

The supplied OpenUsage screenshots are interaction references for the menu bar panel, segmented period control, compact charts, provider reordering, and settings density. They are not pixel-copy targets.

### Product Insight

People using several LLM APIs want cost confidence at a glance, but provider dashboards fragment the truth because each service exposes different metrics and freshness guarantees.

### Design Principles

1. **Truth before symmetry.** Render each provider from its official capabilities; never invent a cost, balance, model breakdown, or real-time promise to make cards look alike.
2. **One-glance hierarchy.** Lead with comparable official spend and freshness, then reveal provider-specific detail. The common task should take seconds.
3. **Calm data density.** Prefer aligned typography, whitespace, and subtle grouping over a wall of equal cards or ornamental effects.
4. **Native interaction.** Follow macOS keyboard, focus, hover, material, drag, menu, and reduced-motion conventions. System typography is intentional here, not a fallback.
5. **Every visual action has an accessible equivalent.** Charts expose summaries and focus/click detail; reordering works through buttons as well as drag; status never relies on color alone.
6. **Delight stays quiet.** Use brief drag lift, smooth state transitions, helpful empty copy, and immediate confirmations. No confetti, bounce, sound, or interaction that delays the financial task.

### Creative Direction

**One sentence:** Four different provider truths collapse into one calm, honest financial glance.

The memorable product signature is capability-aware asymmetry: cost-reporting providers show comparable spend and trends, DeepSeek shows actual funds by currency, and Gemini explicitly shows validated access without pretending that a model list is usage.
